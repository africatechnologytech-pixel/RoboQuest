## RoboQuest Privacy Policy
Last updated: 30 October 2026

## 1. What we collect
- The student's name or nickname.
- An email address. This can be a parent's or guardian's email.
- What you build and earn in the app: projects, points, tags and progress.

We collect nothing else.

## 2. Why we collect it
- To sign you in.
- To keep your work and progress safe, so it is there when you come back.
- To let teachers see who is in their class and how they are doing.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for adverts.

## 3. Where it is kept and who can see it
- It is stored with Firebase, a service from Google.
- Teachers and the app owner can see student names and progress.
- No one else can see it.

## 4. Who can use the app
RoboQuest is for everyone, children and adults. Young children should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

## 5. Payments and your account
- Some access may need a payment. If a payment is not made, the account may be deactivated.
- A deactivated account is not erased. Your information stays, and the account can be reactivated when the payment is made.

## 6. Deleting your account
You can ask for your account and information to be deleted at any time. Ask your teacher or the app owner, and it will be deleted.

## 7. Changes
This policy may change, for example when payments are added. The newest version is always the one shown in the app.
